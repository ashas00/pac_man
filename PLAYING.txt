PAC-MAN  -  42 Vienna
=====================

START
  itch.io app : press "Launch".
  terminal    : ./pac-man config.json

CONTROLS
  Arrows / WASD     move
  P / ESC           pause menu (resume or back to main menu)
  UP/DOWN + ENTER   navigate menus

RULES
  Eat every pacgum to clear a level (10 levels by default).
  Super-pacgums in the corners make ghosts edible for a few seconds.
  Touching a normal ghost, or running out of time, costs a life.
  Score and lives carry over between levels. Enter your name at the end
  to join the top 10.

CHEATS (for evaluation)
  I invincible    Z freeze ghosts    B speed boost
  L skip level    K extra life       F frighten ghosts

CONFIGURATION
  Edit config.json (comments with #, // or /* */ are allowed).
  Main keys: lives, level_count, level_max_time, seed, width, height, pacgum,
  pacgum_percent, player_speed, ghost_speed, points_per_pacgum,
  points_per_super_pacgum, points_per_ghost, frightened_duration,
  ghost_respawn_delay, levels.
  Invalid values are replaced by defaults with a warning in the terminal.
  Highscores are stored in highscores.json next to the game.
